# BoringBots
Building some agents to do tedious things.

## [RedundancyBot](bots/redundancybot/)
RedundancyBot helps you clear out clutter by finding files on your Mac and in Google Drive that are likely duplicates, older versions or convenience copies of one another. It groups candidate files by name and file type, then explains what it found so you can decide what to delete. It never deletes anything itself, so that decision always stays with you.

## [FormBot](bots/formbot/)
FormBot helps you fill out a PDF or Word form one field at a time. It reads the form, looks up anything unclear and shows its sources, then works through each field with you and gives you text to copy in. It never edits the form itself, so you stay in control of what goes on it.
